Overview

X-HViT (Explainable Hybrid Vision Transformer) is an academic research project developed by students of the Bachelor of Computer Engineering program at United Technical College, affiliated to Pokhara University, Nepal.

This platform is provided solely for educational and research demonstration purposes. It is not a certified or approved clinical medical device and must not be used as a substitute for professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice.

Note: By using this platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to these Terms & Conditions in full.
// 02

Medical Disclaimer

⚠ Important: X-HViT outputs are NOT medical diagnoses. All results are AI-generated predictions for research and educational purposes only.

The AI predictions produced by X-HViT, including disease classifications, confidence scores, Grad-CAM heatmaps, and attention maps, are experimental outputs generated by a machine learning model trained on publicly available datasets.

  • Results must not be used to make clinical decisions regarding patient care.
  • All medical images should be reviewed by qualified medical professionals.
  • The platform does not establish any doctor-patient relationship.
  • Developers are not liable for any clinical decisions made based on these outputs.
// 03

Permitted Use

Use of the X-HViT platform is permitted under the following conditions:

  • Academic research and educational demonstration of AI-assisted medical imaging.
  • Testing and evaluation of the hybrid CNN-ViT model architecture.
  • Reviewing explainability outputs (Grad-CAM, attention maps) for learning purposes.
  • Student and faculty research within United Technical College and affiliated institutions.

The following uses are strictly prohibited:

  • Clinical deployment or use in actual patient diagnosis workflows.
  • Commercial distribution or resale of the platform or its outputs.
  • Uploading patient data without proper anonymization and ethical clearance.
  • Reverse engineering the model for competing commercial products.
// 04

Data & Privacy

X-HViT processes images uploaded by users during the demonstration session. The following data practices apply:

  • Uploaded images are processed in-session and not permanently stored on any server.
  • No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected through the platform.
  • Users must not upload identifiable patient data without proper de-identification.
  • Session data may be logged in aggregate for performance monitoring and research improvement.
Best Practice: Always anonymize medical images before uploading. Remove DICOM metadata containing patient names, IDs, or dates of birth.

Research Ethics

X-HViT is developed in accordance with responsible AI research principles:

  • Training data was sourced from publicly available, ethically approved datasets.
  • The model includes explainability features to prevent "black box" decision-making.
  • Bias evaluation across demographic and imaging subgroups is an ongoing research priority.
  • The project follows Pokhara University's research ethics guidelines.
Responsible AI: We are committed to transparent, explainable, and fair AI development. Dual XAI outputs (Grad-CAM + attention maps) are provided to support clinical trust and accountability.
