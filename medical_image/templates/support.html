FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can X-HViT be used for real clinical diagnosis?+
No. X-HViT is an academic research prototype. Its outputs are AI-generated predictions for educational purposes only and must not be used to make clinical decisions. Always consult a qualified medical professional for diagnosis and treatment.
What image formats are supported?+
The platform accepts JPEG (.jpg), PNG (.png), TIFF (.tiff), and DICOM (.dcm) files. All images are internally resized to 224×224 pixels and normalized before inference. Images should be single-channel or RGB; multi-frame DICOM stacks are not supported in the current version.
How does Grad-CAM explainability work?+
Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) uses gradients of the predicted class score flowing back into the final CNN convolutional layer to produce a heatmap highlighting the regions most relevant to the prediction. Higher-intensity (red/yellow) regions indicate higher contribution to the classification decision.
What diseases can X-HViT detect?+
X-HViT currently supports: Brain tumors (Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary) from MRI; Lung adenocarcinoma and nodules from CT; Pneumonia, tuberculosis, fractures from X-ray; and malignant cells, malaria, skin lesions from microscopic imaging. Visit the Modalities page for full details.
Is my uploaded image data stored?+
No. Uploaded images are processed entirely in-session and are not permanently stored on any server. The system does not retain patient data beyond the active browser session. Always anonymize DICOM files before uploading by stripping patient metadata.
What training data was used?+
The model was trained on 28,000+ labeled medical images sourced from Mendeley Data, covering CT, MRI, X-ray, and microscopic modalities. Data was preprocessed with normalization, Gaussian noise filtering, and augmentation (rotation, flipping, zoom) for robust generalization.
How do I download a diagnostic report?+
After running a diagnosis, click the "Full Report" button in the result panel. This takes you to the Report page where you can view the structured diagnostic summary and download it as a PDF. Report generation includes the diagnosis, confidence scores, probability distribution, and Grad-CAM heatmap.
What is the model's accuracy?+
X-HViT targets 95%+ classification accuracy with 93.8% precision, 94.5% recall, and 94.1% F1-score across the test dataset. Performance varies by modality and disease class. See the Metrics page for full evaluation results and evaluation methodology.
