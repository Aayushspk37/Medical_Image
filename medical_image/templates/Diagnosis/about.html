About X-HViT

X-HViT (Explainable Hybrid Vision Transformer) is an academic research project built as the final year BCE project at United Technical College, affiliated to Pokhara University, Nepal.

The goal is to bridge the gap between high-accuracy medical AI and clinical interpretability — providing both powerful predictions and transparent visual explanations that clinicians can trust.
