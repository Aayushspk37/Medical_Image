How It Works

Four steps from image upload to clinical-grade diagnostic report.

📤

Upload Image

Drag-and-drop or browse to upload CT, MRI, X-ray, or microscopic image. Select modality and disease category.

⚡

AI Inference

X-HViT processes the image through CNN feature extraction, patch embedding, and transformer attention in <3 seconds.

🔬

XAI Overlay

Grad-CAM heatmaps and attention maps generated to highlight the regions driving the classification decision.

📄

Export Report

Download a structured PDF diagnostic report with results, heatmaps, confidence scores, and case metadata.
