Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about X-HViT, medical imaging AI, and the platform.

Can X-HViT be used for real clinical diagnosis?+
No. X-HViT is an academic research prototype. Its outputs are AI-generated predictions for educational purposes only and must not be used to make clinical decisions. Always consult a qualified medical professional.
What image formats are supported?+
JPEG (.jpg), PNG (.png), TIFF (.tiff), and DICOM (.dcm) formats are supported. All images are resized to 224×224px and normalized before inference. Multi-frame DICOM stacks are not supported in the current version.
How does Grad-CAM explainability work?+
Grad-CAM backpropagates the gradient of the predicted class score through the final CNN convolutional layer to produce a heatmap. High-intensity regions (red/yellow) indicate higher importance to the classification. This makes the model's spatial reasoning transparent to clinicians.
Why use both CNN and Vision Transformer?+
CNNs excel at extracting local spatial features (edges, textures, micro-lesions) while Vision Transformers capture long-range global dependencies across the full image. Combining both gives X-HViT superior representational power compared to either architecture alone — especially important for complex medical pathologies.
What diseases can X-HViT detect?+
X-HViT supports: Brain tumors (Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary) from MRI; Lung adenocarcinoma and nodules from CT; Pneumonia, tuberculosis, fractures from X-ray; and malignant cells, malaria, skin lesions from microscopic imaging.
Is my uploaded image data stored?+
No. Uploaded images are processed in-session and not permanently stored on any server. Always anonymize DICOM files before uploading by stripping patient metadata (names, IDs, dates).
What training data was used?+
28,000+ labeled medical images from Mendeley Data, an open-access research repository. Data spans CT, MRI, X-ray, and microscopic modalities and was preprocessed with normalization, Gaussian filtering, and augmentation.
What is the model's accuracy?+
X-HViT targets 95%+ classification accuracy with 93.8% precision, 94.5% recall, and 94.1% F1-score. Performance varies by modality. See the Metrics page for full details.
What tech stack does X-HViT use?+
PyTorch for deep learning, Hugging Face Transformers for ViT modules, Grad-CAM library for explainability, OpenCV for image preprocessing, Python + Django for backend, SQLite3 for storage, Google Colab for GPU training, Scikit-learn for evaluation, and Matplotlib/Seaborn for visualization.
How do I download a diagnostic report?+
After running a diagnosis, click "Full Report" in the result panel. The Report page lets you view the structured diagnostic summary and download it as a PDF. Reports include diagnosis, confidence, probability distribution, and Grad-CAM heatmap.
Is X-HViT free to use?+
Yes, X-HViT is free for academic and research use. It is a non-commercial academic project with no subscription fees. See the Pricing page for details on future plans if the platform is expanded.
