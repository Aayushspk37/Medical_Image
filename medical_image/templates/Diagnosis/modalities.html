Dataset Breakdown

Mendeley Data Repository

All training data is sourced from Mendeley Data, a curated open-access repository for research datasets. Images span four distinct imaging modalities with class-balanced distributions for unbiased model training.

Data preprocessing includes resizing to 224×224, min-max normalization, Gaussian noise filtering, and augmentation strategies to maximize generalization.

Preprocessing

Data Augmentation

Comprehensive augmentation pipeline ensures the model generalizes beyond training distribution.

01

Resize & Normalize

All images resized to 224×224px and pixel values min-max normalized to [0,1] range for stable gradient flow.

02

Gaussian Noise Filter

Gaussian smoothing reduces acquisition noise common in clinical imaging equipment, improving feature clarity.

03

Random Rotation

Images are rotated ±30° during training to enforce rotational invariance for varied patient orientations.

04

Horizontal Flip

Random horizontal flipping doubles effective dataset size and prevents directional bias in anatomical features.

05

Zoom & Crop

Random zoom (0.8–1.2×) and center cropping simulate different scanner distances and field-of-view settings.

06

Brightness / Contrast

Random brightness and contrast jitter of ±20% simulates different imaging conditions across clinical sites.
