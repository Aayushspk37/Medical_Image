Overview

X-HViT Medical is an academic research platform developed by BCE students at United Technical College. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our diagnostic AI system.

Research Context: X-HViT is a non-commercial academic project. We do not sell data, run advertisements, or share personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.
// 02

Data We Collect

When you use X-HViT, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, institutional role (for registered users)
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, session duration
  • Uploaded medical images (processed in-session only — see Section 5)
  • Device and browser information for performance monitoring
  • Feedback and support messages submitted via the contact form
// 03

How We Use Data

  • To provide AI-assisted diagnostic analysis and generate reports
  • To improve model accuracy and platform performance through aggregated analytics
  • To respond to support requests and feedback
  • To maintain platform security and prevent misuse
  • For academic research publication (aggregate, anonymized data only)
// 04

Data Storage & Security

X-HViT stores minimal personal data. Account credentials are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt encryption. Diagnostic session data is stored in SQLite3 on secure servers.

  • Passwords are never stored in plaintext
  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Session tokens are invalidated on logout
  • You may request account deletion at any time via the contact form
// 05

Medical Image Privacy

Important: Uploaded medical images are processed entirely in-session and are NOT permanently stored on our servers. Images are discarded immediately after inference.

Users must ensure any uploaded medical images are properly anonymized. Do not upload images containing patient names, IDs, or other personally identifiable information in DICOM metadata or visible in the image.

// 06

Cookies

X-HViT uses minimal, strictly necessary cookies for session management (keeping you logged in). We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or analytics cookies from third parties.

// 07

Third-Party Services

X-HViT uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (no personal data transmitted)
  • Google Colab — for model training (no user data used in training)

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising networks.

// 08

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export: Download your diagnostic history and reports
  • Opt-out: Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing

To exercise any right, contact us at xhvit@utc.edu.np.

// 09

Children's Privacy

X-HViT is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us immediately.
