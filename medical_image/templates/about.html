About X-HViT

X-HViT (Explainable Hybrid Vision Transformer) is an academic research project built as the final year BCE project at United Technical College, affiliated to Pokhara University, Nepal.

The goal is to bridge the gap between high-accuracy medical AI and clinical interpretability — providing both powerful predictions and transparent visual explanations that clinicians can trust.

Mission

Why We Built X-HViT

🔍
Explainability First
Every prediction includes Grad-CAM and transformer attention maps so clinicians can understand and verify the model's reasoning.
🌐
Multi-Modal Scope
One unified framework handles CT, MRI, X-ray, and Microscopic imaging without needing modality-specific models.
⚡
Deployable Design
Optimized architecture enables deployment in resource-constrained clinical environments without expensive GPU infrastructure.
📖
Open Research
Built on open-source tools and trained on publicly available datasets to encourage reproducibility and further research.
Journey

Project Timeline

01
September 2025

Project Proposal

Initial proposal submitted to the Department of Computer Engineering. Scope, objectives, and dataset selection finalized.

02
October 2025

Dataset Collection

28,000+ medical images curated from Mendeley Data. Preprocessing pipeline developed with OpenCV.

03
November 2025

Architecture Design

Hybrid CNN-ViT architecture designed and implemented in PyTorch with patch embedding and MHSA modules.

04
December 2025

Training & XAI

Model trained on Google Colab GPU. Grad-CAM and transformer attention map modules integrated.

05
January 2026

Evaluation & Web UI

K-fold cross-validation completed. Django backend and clinical web interface built with full report generation.

06
February 2026

Final Submission

Project submitted to the Department of Computer Engineering, United Technical College. Thesis, demo, and documentation delivered.
